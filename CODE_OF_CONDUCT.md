# Code of Conduct

This is a small teaching-focused list, and the rules are correspondingly simple.

## Our standard

- **Be respectful.** Critique suggestions and links, never people. No harassment, insults, or
  personal attacks of any kind.
- **Be honest.** Only describe resources you have actually opened. Never claim a link works if
  you have not checked it.
- **Respect ownership.** Do not suggest resources that redistribute other people's copyrighted
  work without permission, and do not copy other lists' descriptions or structure into this one.
- **Stay on topic.** Suggestions must serve civil engineering teaching or learning.

## Responsibilities

The maintainer ([Syed Rafi Ahmed](https://github.com/SyedRafiAhmedGIS)) may edit, reject, or
remove any contribution, and may ban accounts that repeatedly violate this code.

## Reporting

Report a problem by opening a GitHub issue, or by contacting the maintainer through the GitHub
profile above. Reports will be reviewed and answered; repeated or serious violations lead to
removal and, where warranted, a public note in the relevant issue.
